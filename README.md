# 📖 Overview

**Sanket Notes** is a modern, privacy-first, offline-first note-taking application designed for students, professionals, researchers, creators, businesses, and government organizations.

Built with simplicity, speed, and security at its core, Sanket Notes helps you capture ideas, manage knowledge, organize projects, and stay productive—without compromising your privacy.

Unlike many note-taking applications, Sanket Notes does not require mandatory sign-ups or constant internet connectivity. Your notes stay on your device, under your control.

> **Your Notes. Your Device. Your Privacy.**

---

# 🌍 Vision

Our vision is to build a next-generation knowledge management platform that combines the ease of traditional note-taking with modern AI-powered productivity.

Sanket Notes is more than just a notebook. It is your personal digital workspace.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Privacy is the foundation of Sanket Notes.

Features include:

* Offline First
* No Mandatory Login
* Local Storage
* App Lock
* Fingerprint Unlock
* Face Unlock (Supported Devices)
* Encrypted Local Database
* Secure Backup
* Optional End-to-End Encrypted Sync (Future)

No tracking.

No analytics.

No hidden uploads.

No unnecessary permissions.
